# Pickup, Warehouse, and Drop-Off Locations

Buttons Bebe operates out of a **warehouse** (not a traditional retail store), but customers are welcome to visit during hours to browse, try things on, pick up orders, or drop off returns.

---

## Locations and hours

| Location | Address | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| **Warehouse — the local warehouse city** | the store warehouse address | Mon–Thu 9am–3pm · Fri 9am–2pm |
| **after-hours return drop-off** | the published after-hours return drop-off, side door (return bin) | 24/7 |
| **local pickup bin** | Outdoor bin at the warehouse address | 24/7 |

Extended warehouse hours are sometimes available — customers should confirm hours before visiting (see contacts below).

---

## Order pickup

When placing an order, the customer selects the local pickup option at checkout. The order is placed in the **outdoor pickup bin at the warehouse, accessible 24/7**. Orders are held in the bin for approximately **2 months**.

**Customer response (pickup ready):**
> Hi! Your order is ready for pickup at the store warehouse address. It's in our outdoor pickup bin, which is accessible 24 hours a day, so you can come by anytime. Orders stay in the bin for about 2 months. If you have any trouble finding it, just let us know!

---

## Returns drop-off

Returns can be dropped off in person (no label needed) at either:
- **the published after-hours return drop-off, side door, Lakewood** — 24/7 return bin
- **the store warehouse address** — during warehouse hours

**Customer response (drop-off options):**
> Hi! You're welcome to drop off your return at our 24/7 return bin at the published after-hours return drop-off (side door) at the after-hours drop-off, or at our warehouse at the store warehouse address during hours (Mon–Thu 9am–3pm, Fri 9am–2pm). No label needed for drop-off.

---

## Visiting to try items on

Customers are welcome to come to the warehouse during hours to try items before buying.

**Customer response (try-on visit):**
> Hi! You're welcome to stop by our warehouse to try things on: the store warehouse address (Mon–Thu 9am–3pm, Fri 9am–2pm). We sometimes have extended hours, so feel free to confirm hours before your visit. See you soon!

---

## Contacts

Customer service is primarily handled by **email** so inquiries can be tracked and answered thoroughly. The following lines are available for specific needs:

| Purpose | Contact |
|---|---|
| Customer service (primary) | Email — see store website |
| Warehouse hours / new arrivals | Text / WhatsApp **555-0100** |
| Pickup questions | Text **555-0101** |
| General phone | **555-0102** (confirm current status with team) |
| Escalation — manager | **555-0199** — confirm with the team before sharing |

> **Note for the agent:** The manager's cell (555-0199) must only be shared with a customer after confirming with the team. Do not distribute it automatically. See [Core Rules](../core-rules.md) on escalation.
